Ecosystem Features

Store Sync

Native integration with Heroic Games Launcher to automatically pull in your GOG and Epic libraries with the correct launch commands, plus Steam API fetching.

Local First

No accounts required. All your data, covers, and trailers are stored locally in a single, portable folder. Easily backup and restore your entire setup via ZIP.

Data Nerd Heaven

Integrated HowLongToBeat and ProtonDB fetching. Export your entire library to a spreadsheet, or batch-import a CSV to populate your library instantly.
