CAFE NEUROTICO - System Origin

SYSTEM ORIGIN: HUMAN BRAIN + MACHINE

FULL DISCLOSURE: AI ARCHITECTURE

I am an architect, not a traditional developer. Every single line of code in Cafe Neurotico was generated, assembled, and debugged using advanced AI assistance.

I provided the neurotic vision, the logic, and the UI/UX design. The AI wrote the syntax.

Built to solve a personal obsession with library management.

“NO MAN IS BETTER THAN A MACHINE, BUT NO MACHINE IS BETTER THAN A MAN WITH A MACHINE” — Paul Tudor Jones

Although it would be tempting to think that I gave a prompt like “Make an app like this” or “Make a copy of the whatever software”, and it auto-magically just worked, it couldn’t be further from the truth. Try it for yourself.

It’s a very personal project that started just like a way to manage my own Excel CSV games file (which was huge BTW), it was a just a Python script, then I added a native interface using Qt libraries, it was nice, but then I remembered that I like to distro-hop and Qt can look ugly in different environments, so I moved to Electron, then I’ve made many incremental changes, as I was having more and more ideas of what I wanted. Changes that sometimes broke the whole thing. Then, talking to my true-developer friends, I’ve changed my AI assistant and started using GitHub instead of doing everything locally using simple text editors (Yeah, I’m that old...). The logical solutions came from me burning my neurons, like using the Steam API, implementing the Heroic Sync or solving ProtonDB and HLTB. AI still needs someone to tell it what to do.

It has been a journey. It’s been a couple of months now and I’ve covered a massive amount of ground, from the initial idea, the UI design, the database logic, and drafting the manual—we are easily looking at hundreds of hours for the entire project. Not to mention more hours of researching for solutions. I’ve been putting in some serious architectural work to build this. Also keep in mind that I am a 47yo father and husband who works as a teacher from 7am to 9pm on weekdays, so I don’t have a ton of time to do it.

I would like to encourage people who have ideas to put them into practice. I know I don’t have the time to do thousands of lines of code, I just don’t, but this is not going to stop me from being creative.

I like to think of AI as an employee, or even a team that I have at my disposal to execute my ideas, so I have more headspace to create them.

If I can conceptualize and architect something, like a building, but I’m not the one laying brick by brick, does it make me less of a creator? I mean, people remember the ones who conceived things, not the ones who actually performed the action of building them. It is what it is.

I’ve been gaming since the 80’s and using Linux since 2002, my first distro was Mandrake Linux, from a CD inserted in a magazine. I’ve been around and I’ve seen distros being born and dying. The first Ubuntu, the first Live-CDs (Knoppix, Kurumin, Kalango), the last Mandrake, Conectiva (they merged into Mandriva), Yellow Dog (PPC architecture) oh boy... Good memories! I’ve done some coding decades ago (even Cobol, but don’t ask, I remember nothing lol) I will keep on using CNGM to manage my library. This is a labor of love, for gaming and for Linux. Cheers!

J.R.A.
